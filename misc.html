Interests

I love outdoor adventures. Living in the Pacific Northwest makes that easy—hiking, camping, backpacking, skiing, and water sports are all close by. What I chase most is pure wilderness experiences: long days off-trail, sometimes 20+ hours and 25 miles through empty mountains, with nobody else around and no trails underfoot. The landscape feels like a puzzle, and you never know what comes next. I thrive in that solitude, with its mix of refreshment, doubt, fear, and freedom.

I keep a record of my trips here, many of which are about the great outdoors.

I also stay at home a lot. I spend my time with music, film, and literature.

  • Music. I listen widely but pickily: classical (especially Bruckner and Sibelius), progressive rock (Genesis, Pink Floyd), experimental and fusion work, electronic (trance, melodic and synth-leaning stuff, ambient), and international pop. I play electric guitar and bass—one more emotional, the other more fun—and hope to pick up drums someday. Dream instrument: the synthesizer.
  • Film. Movies let you live other lives for a couple of hours. Favorites include Catch Me If You Can and The Lord of the Rings series. I like Ridley Scott (Blade Runner, Alien, Prometheus) and Christopher Nolan (Inception, Memento). Beyond cinematography and emotional delivery, I care about films that stay complex and open to interpretation. I side with Scorsese: superhero movies aren't cinema. I'm never a fan of Marvel movies.
  • Literature. I've read classics since high school and still try to keep the habit. V. S. Naipaul is a favorite—he blurs fiction and nonfiction, and I like how observant and sharp his writing is, especially around postcolonial themes. Two books I love that couldn't be more different: Night Flight and Nostromo. Since 2023 I've also been reading some self-empowerment books for personal growth.

I used to be a collector of many—miniature vehicles, hi-fi equipment, fountain pens, playing cards, (neo) vintage watches—but I've mostly let that go for a simpler, more practical life.

Still on the to-do list: cooking, making electronic music, fishing, diving, sailing, dancing, oil painting, restoring antiques. There is never enough time to try everything.

Easy conversation starters: cars (big Top Gear fan), dinosaurs, sandwiches (burgers, gyros, burritos, you name it), or throw me a hot take like something from r/unpopularopinion. Odds are decent I'll agree with you.
